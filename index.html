Dead Village

Cross the fields, the pine forest and the village to ring the bell in the old tower. The villagers are infected. Some are asleep on their feet, some patrol. Avoid them or put them down.

Left thumb: walk (push fully to run). Move the phone to look, or drag on the right. Crouch in tall grass to vanish. Throw a stone to lure them away. Sneak behind one for a silent takedown. Gunshots wake everyone nearby.

Keyboard: WASD walk, Shift run, drag to look, Space fire, C crouch, Q stone, L light, E action, R reload.
